SIFT, first 100,000 vectors, 128 dimensions, M = 16, efConstruction = 200

| Step | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build in memory | 32.7 s | no durability |
| Build with every write logged, one fsync at the end | 33.2 s | log is 51 MB |
| Recover by replaying the log | 33.3 s | rebuilds the graph insert by insert |
| Write a snapshot | 0.11 s | 58 MB, includes fsync |
| Recover from the snapshot | 0.09 s | 368x faster than replay; results identical to the in-memory build: true |

Log appends alone (2,000 records of 128 floats): 1,771 per second with an fsync per record, 257,526 per second without.
